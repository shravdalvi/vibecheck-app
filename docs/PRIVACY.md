# Vibecheck Privacy Policy

Event staff can see your anonymous location on a live map during the event. Other attendees never can.

## What we collect
- Location (GPS) during the event
- Movement intensity
- Battery level
- Connection status

## What we DO NOT collect
- Your name
- Your contacts
- Your photos
- Your microphone data
- Your messages

## Data Deletion
All data is deleted after the event ends. You can also request data deletion from the Profile screen at any time.
